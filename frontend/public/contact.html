BEFORE YOU WRITE

A few things worth knowing.

What happens after you send this

Someone from the care team reads every enquiry personally. You will hear back within one working day with a real next step, not an automated sequence.

What we will ask you

Only what helps us point you somewhere useful — roughly what you are dealing with, how you would prefer to meet, and when you are free.

How your details are handled

Your enquiry is confidential from the moment it arrives. It is never shared outside the care team and never used for marketing.

If you need help right now

Sora Health does not provide crisis services. If you are in immediate danger, contact your local emergency number or a crisis line straight away.
